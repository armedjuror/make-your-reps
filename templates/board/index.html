Everything you need to make your reps

One board. No clutter. Just the tools that move the needle.

Habit Tracker with Streaks

Build lasting habits with daily check-ins, visual streak tracking, frequency scheduling, and optional accountability partners who get notified on your progress.

Smart Todo Manager

Organise tasks into groups, set deadlines, filter by status or date, and tick them off — every completed task earns you XP and improves your daily productivity score.

Built-in Pomodoro Timer

Stay in flow with a customisable Pomodoro timer — set focus duration, short break, and long break. Each completed session is tracked and rewarded with points.

Daily Journal

Write freely in a distraction-free journal editor. A monthly calendar shows which days you've written, and a gentle reminder appears on your daily timeline.

AI Productivity Assistant

Ask your AI assistant anything — it knows your habits, tasks, and sleep data. It can create todos, motivate you, and help you reflect on your progress.

Gamification & Achievements

Earn XP for every habit check, completed task, journal entry, and Pomodoro session. Level up through named ranks, maintain daily streaks, and unlock 29 badges.

Daily Timeline

A live timeline shows your schedule for the day — habit reminders, task deadlines, routine entries, sleep logs, and journal prompts, all in chronological order.

Reading List & Bookmarks

Save websites and resources to a quick-access bookmark grid on your home screen. Tap any bookmark to open it instantly — no digging through browser tabs.

Routine & Sleep Tracker

Build a structured daily routine with timed activities, and log your sleep each morning. See your sleep pattern charted over the last two weeks at a glance.
